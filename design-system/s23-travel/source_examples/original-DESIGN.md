## Brand & Style

This design system translates the experience of curated journeys, international family travel, and bespoke tourism between South America and Europe into an approachable, high-trust digital environment. The visual language blends the security and poise of deep oceanic navies with the vibrancy, human warmth, and optimism of sun-drenched terracotta and sky blues.

### Personality & Values
- **Careful & Trustworthy:** Reliable travel orchestration, peace of mind, transparent pricing, and attentive personal concierge service.
- **Warm & Inspiring:** Sunlit destinations, family discovery, hospitality, and unforgettable human connections.
- **Modern Editorial:** Crisp geometric typography paired with graceful display serifs for aspirational narrative moments and travel storytelling.

### Visual Aesthetic
A balanced hybrid of **Modern Corporate Humanism** and **Aspirational Travel Editorial**:
- Clean white surface foundations accented with structured navy navigation bars and dark night sky thematic sections.
- Pill-shaped interaction targets providing welcoming, tactile touchpoints.
- Warm terracotta pill tags, photo overlays, and energetic call-to-actions that invite action without inducing stress.
- Rich photography presented inside gently rounded framing containers with soft environmental elevations.

## Colors

The color palette captures two primary sensations: the reassuring dependability of deep maritime blues and the joyful excitement of departure gates and golden-hour skies.

### Hierarchy & Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#D4641C` - Vibrant Terracotta / Orange):** Used for primary conversion triggers, essential highlights, active badge fills, and highlighted headline spans.
- **Secondary (`#101D42` - Deep Navy):** Anchors headers, primary headers, global navigation headers, footers, and foundational typographic body copy for high-contrast legibility.
- **Tertiary (`#A3CBE1` - Sky Light Blue):** Serves as an atmospheric tint, background shading for flight tags, icon backgrounds, and gentle interactive states.
- **Neutral (`#C7CED0` - Soft Slate Grey):** Defines quiet border structures, dividers, card boundaries, and inactive pagination indicators.

### Extended Brand Accents
- `brand-coral-bright` (`#FC7C3F`): Hover and focus glow states for CTA buttons, promotional banners, and urgent callouts.
- `brand-navy-rich` (`#162E61`) & `brand-midnight` (`#152340`): Used for tonal shifts across complex footers, navigation sub-menus, and layered card backdrops.
- `surface-bg` (`#F8FAFB`) & `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Light-mode foundation surfaces ensuring clean contrast and optical breathing room.
- `whatsapp-green` (`#25D366`): Dedicated floating instant-assistance target anchored to the bottom corner.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes clear visual pacing across narrative storytelling, commercial travel offers, and technical itineraries using the Poppins family for a cohesive, modern geometric aesthetic.

### Font Roles
- **Headlines & Section Titles (Poppins):** An expressive, contemporary geometric sans-serif that captures structured boldness and high warmth across all screen sizes.
- **Storytelling & Editorial Campaigns (Poppins):** Applied uniformly to maintain clean, modern legibility across inspirational hero titles and travel memoirs.
- **Body & Controls (Poppins):** A friendly, highly readable sans-serif that keeps dense travel descriptions, itineraries, inputs, and button pills clear and approachable.

### Scaling & Rules
- Large headings scaled down systematically on mobile via dedicated `*-mobile` token pairs.
- For headline spans emphasizing reassurance (e.g. *"com o cuidado que você merece"*), switch the font color token directly to `brand-terracotta` while preserving the parent weight.
- Maintain consistent geometric styling across all form controls, search inputs, and card metadata tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a responsive 12-column grid system designed to support destination showcases, editorial itineraries, and service propositions.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, max container width of `1240px` centered with variable margins. Destination catalogs utilize a 3-column card arrangement (`col-span-4` each).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column layout, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins. Destination catalogs collapse into a 2-column format (`col-span-4` each).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` margins. Filters, form bars, and cards reflow into a single column (`col-span-4`).

### Spacing Rhythm
- Section vertical rhythm adheres to `space-2xl` (`4rem`) on desktop and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) on mobile.
- Card padding is set to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) internally, with card header media maintaining flush bleeds to the top, left, and right borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids aggressive artificial dropshadows, favoring sun-lit ambient dispersion and subtle borders that preserve clean daylight clarity.

### Depth Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to canvas backgrounds (`surface-bg`) and static structural panels.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest):** Subtle border `1px solid rgba(199, 206, 208, 0.45)` paired with a soft ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(16, 29, 66, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Popovers):** Applied when hovering destination cards and active dropdown selects: `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(16, 29, 66, 0.12)`, accompanied by a slight `-2px` vertical translation.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navigation & Floating CTAs):** Applied to the fixed WhatsApp support trigger and floating mobile nav bar: `0 8px 28px rgba(16, 29, 66, 0.16)`.
- **Hero Image Overlays:** Scrim gradients use `#101D42` at 65% base opacity fading to 25%, ensuring crisp text contrast on photo backdrops without flattening imagery.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Pill-shaped (Level 3)** roundedness strategy to mirror the fluid, approachable curves of the brand mark and convey friendly travel hospitality.

### Corner Radius Hierarchy
- **Pill Targets (`rounded-full` / `9999px`):** Primary action buttons, destination pill badges, header badges ("PRESENTES NO BRASIL & PORTUGAL"), and floating WhatsApp launcher.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` to `1.25rem`):** Destination cards, search filter boxes, itinerary timeline blocks, and testimonial containers.
- **Form Controls (`rounded-md` / `0.5rem` to `0.75rem`):** Destination search inputs, date pickers, and select dropdowns maintain slightly firmer corners for reliable usability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** High-contrast background in `brand-terracotta` (`#D4641C`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, full pill radius (`9999px`), horizontal padding `space-lg`, vertical padding `space-sm`. Hover states shift to `brand-coral-bright` (`#FC7C3F`) with subtle ambient shadow.
- **Secondary Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface with `1.5px solid #FFFFFF` on dark sections or `1.5px solid #101D42` on light sections. Hover fills with 10% white or 5% navy tint.
- **Icon Buttons:** Circular pill buttons (`44px x 44px`) housing inline travel iconography (e.g. arrow link `→`, plane icon).

### Badges & Category Chips
- **Destination Badges:** Floating pill tags positioned over image cards (top-left offset `16px`). Background `brand-terracotta` (`#D4641C`) with white label text, tracking `+0.04em`, `padding: 4px 12px`.
- **Regional Pill Indicator:** Deep slate/navy frosted background with a terracotta dot indicator (e.g., `● PRESENTES NO BRASIL & PORTUGAL`).

### Destination & Package Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical composition with a 16:10 aspect ratio image bleed on top, `1rem` inner padding below for destination location (`title-card`), description copy (`body-sm`), and a bottom link anchor: *"Conhecer destino →"* in terracotta.
- **Border:** `1px solid rgba(199, 206, 208, 0.5)` with `16px` border-radius and hidden overflow to crop imagery cleanly.

### Form Inputs & Search Filters
- **Filter Bar:** Multi-segment horizontal container housed on clean white surfaces. Input field for text search ("Pesquisar destinos...") accompanied by dropdown filters (Regions, Sort Order).
- **Control Styling:** Light background, subtle slate border (`#C7CED0`), `8px` corner radius, font size `14px`, focus state ring in `brand-terracotta` (`2px solid rgba(212, 100, 28, 0.25)`).

### Navigation & Footer
- **Global Header:** Deep Navy (`#101D42`) backdrop, brand cloud icon with orange logotype on the left, clear white navigation links (`#FFFFFF`), and prominent CTA pill button on the right.
- **Footer:** Deep Navy foundation with clean typographic columns: Brand premise & CADASTUR certification, Quick links, Contact details with international dial codes (+351 / +55), and social channel links.

### Floating Concierge Launcher
- Bottom-right fixed circular target (`56px x 56px`) in `whatsapp-green` (`#25D366`) featuring a crisp white chat bubble glyph, anchored at `24px` from the bottom and right edges with Level 3 elevation.
